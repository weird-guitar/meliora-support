What it does

Everything you record, kept within reach

Meliora handles the whole path from the first word to the moment you want to find it again.

R

Record with confidence

Begin recording right away, watch a Live Draft while you speak, and add markers to the moments that matter. Recording can continue in the background or with the screen locked.

T

Read and revisit

Follow the transcript as audio plays, tap any segment to jump to that moment, separate distinct speakers, and rename them throughout a transcript.

O

Stay organized

Group related recordings into collections, add tags and favorites, and search titles, transcripts, and tags. Deleted entries stay recoverable for a limited time.

E

Export and back up

Share the original audio, export transcripts in useful formats, and create portable library backups. Protect backups with a password.

Built for privacy

Your recordings stay on your iPhone

Meliora is designed so your library remains yours.

No account required

You don’t need to sign up or sign in. Recordings and transcripts remain on your device unless you explicitly share, export, or back them up.

Local speech processing

Supported transcription and speaker-processing features use models installed locally on your iPhone.

Protected access

Optionally require Face ID or your device passcode. Authentication is handled by iOS, and Meliora does not receive or store biometric data.
