Meliora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Meliora is designed to keep your recordings and personal reflections under your control. This policy explains how Meliora handles information.

1. No Account Required

Meliora does not require you to create an account or sign in.

2. Data Collection

Meliora does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, recordings, transcripts, speaker labels, contacts, location information, or usage history.

Meliora does not sell personal information and does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

3. Recordings and Microphone Access

Microphone access is used only to support recording features initiated by the user.

Meliora supports user-initiated background recording. Recording continues only after the user starts a recording in the app.

4. Local Speech Processing

Transcription, translation, and speaker-processing features are designed to run locally using models installed on the device.

Some speech or speaker-processing models may need to be downloaded before these features can be used. After installation, supported processing is performed locally.

Speaker separation distinguishes voices in a recording. It does not identify the real-world identity of a speaker, and its results may require correction.

5. Face ID and Device Authentication

If the privacy lock is enabled, Meliora uses authentication provided by iOS to protect access to the app.

Meliora does not receive or store facial data, fingerprints, device passcodes, or other biometric information.

6. Local Storage

Recordings, transcripts, speaker labels, markers, collections, tags, favorites, recently deleted entries, and preferences are stored within Meliora’s local app container.

Depending on the user’s device and iCloud settings, locally stored app data may be included in a device or iCloud backup managed by Apple.

7. Sharing and Export

Information leaves Meliora only when the user explicitly chooses to share, export, or back it up through a destination provided by iOS.

Meliora does not control the privacy or storage practices of destinations selected by the user.

8. Backups

Meliora supports standard and encrypted library backups.

Warning: Standard backups are not encrypted by Meliora. Store them only in trusted locations.

Encrypted backups use password-based encryption. Meliora cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

Previously exported backups are separate copies. Deleting a recording from Meliora does not delete copies that were already exported or backed up.

9. Data Deletion

Users can move recordings to Recently Deleted and permanently remove them from within Meliora.

Deleting the app removes locally stored Meliora data from the device unless it was separately exported, backed up, or retained through an Apple-managed device backup.

10. Children’s Privacy

Meliora is a general-purpose productivity application and is not directed specifically at children. Meliora does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Meliora’s features or legal requirements change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact:
