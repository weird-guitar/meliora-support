Meliora Support

Help with recording, offline models, privacy, recovery, playback, and protected backups.

Contact

For support, feedback, or feature requests, contact:

meliora.app@proton.me

To help us respond quickly, please include:

  • your iPhone model
  • your iOS version
  • the Meliora version and build number
  • what you expected to happen
  • what happened instead
  • whether the issue can be reproduced

Privacy warning: Do not send private recordings, transcripts, backup passwords, Apple credentials, or other sensitive information unless you intentionally choose to share them.

Recording

  1. Open Meliora.
  2. Start a new reflection.
  3. Grant microphone access when requested.
  4. Speak normally and add markers when useful.
  5. Tap Stop & Save.

Meliora supports user-initiated background recording and screen-lock recording. Once you start a recording, it can continue while the app is in the background or the screen is locked.

Live Draft

Live Draft may take some time to appear while a local model is prepared. Audio recording begins independently of Live Draft and should continue even if Live Draft is unavailable.

Offline Models

Some features require downloaded local models. Downloading a model requires an internet connection, and the device needs enough free storage to install it.

If a model is missing or damaged, you can check it and repair it from Settings.

Speaker Separation

Speaker separation labels distinct voices as Speaker 1, Speaker 2, and so on. It does not identify real people.

Accuracy can be affected by overlapping voices, background noise, distance from the microphone, and voices that sound similar. You can rename speakers and correct results where needed.

Playback

  • Drag the playback timeline to move to any point in the recording.
  • Use the waveform to navigate by sound.
  • Seek 15 seconds backward or forward with the skip controls.
  • If you cannot hear audio, check the audio route and volume.
  • If you use headphones, disconnect and reconnect them if needed.

Backup and Restore

  • Standard backups are not encrypted by Meliora. Store them only in trusted locations.
  • Encrypted backups use the password you select.
  • Meliora cannot recover a forgotten encrypted-backup password.
  • An incorrect password should not change your existing library.

Recording Recovery

If the app closes before a recording finishes saving, reopening Meliora may offer recovery. Use recovery when it appears to keep the audio from the unfinished recording.

Known beta instruction. If Stop & Save repeatedly fails:

  1. Do not begin another recording.
  2. Close and reopen Meliora.
  3. Use recording recovery when offered.

Recently Deleted

Deleted recordings may remain in Recently Deleted until you permanently remove them. Permanent deletion cannot be reversed unless you have a separate backup.

Privacy Lock

You can optionally require Face ID or your device passcode to open Meliora. Authentication is handled by iOS, and Meliora does not receive or store biometric data.

If Recording Does Not Begin

  • Confirm that microphone access is allowed for Meliora.
  • Confirm that the device has sufficient storage.
  • Close other apps that may be using the microphone.
  • Restart Meliora.
